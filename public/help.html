How to make your invitation

Everything happens in your editor. Change anything, any time; guests see it the next time they open the link.

1. Open your editor

Tap the editor link we sent you. Or open the editor and type your PIN (8 letters and numbers, like ABCD-EFGH). Your phone remembers it, so next time the editor opens straight away.

2. Names and hero

Type the names, the main date and time (the countdown counts down to it), a short date line and your message. Type in the English box; the Tamil box fills in by itself a second later. Check it, and change it if you like.

Tap அ Fill missing Tamil at the bottom to fill every empty Tamil box at once.

3. Photos and music

  • Cover photo: a landscape photo works best. Use JPG or PNG (iPhone HEIC photos can't be read; send them to yourself on WhatsApp first, which turns them into JPG).
  • Music: choose a song, drag the gold handles to the part you want, tap Preview, then Use this clip.

4. Events

Add each ceremony (for example Reception and Muhurtham) with its date, time, venue and address. Put the venue's Google Maps link in, and guests get a Get Directions button.

5. Help and contacts

A message for guests travelling from out of town, and the people guests can call. Each number becomes a tap-to-call button.

6. Design

Pick the design you like. Preview this event in the selected template shows your own details in it.

7. Links

You can have up to three links, each in its own design. For each link, choose what it shows. For example, keep contact numbers on the link for family and hide them on the link for friends.

8. Save and preview

Tap Save changes, then View site to see your invitation as guests will.

9. Pay and share

Pay ₹200 using the button at the top of your editor, and send us the screenshot on WhatsApp. Once we confirm it, your link opens to guests. Tap Copy link and share it on WhatsApp.

10. Wishes

Guests' wishes appear in the Wishes tab. Hide or delete any of them; it changes straight away.

Stuck? Message us on WhatsApp with your link name.
