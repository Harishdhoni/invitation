Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 13 October 2026

Try before you pay

You can fill in your whole invitation and preview it before paying, so you only pay when you're happy with it. If you decide not to go ahead before paying, there is nothing to cancel; just let us know and we'll delete it.

When we refund

  • If it doesn't work because of us and we can't fix it, we refund you in full.
  • If you paid twice or paid the wrong amount, we refund the extra.
  • If you cancel within 48 hours of paying and before your event, we refund you in full.

After that, because the invitation has already been made and opened to your guests, we don't refund it.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the account or UPI ID you paid from, within 5–7 working days of our agreeing to them.

How to ask

Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your invitation's link and your payment reference.
