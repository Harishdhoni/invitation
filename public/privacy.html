Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

This policy explains what we (“we”) collect when you buy an invitation website or open one as a guest, what we do with it, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • From customers: your name and phone number, the payment amount, and the payment reference from your UPI app. We never see your card, bank or UPI PIN details.
  • What you put on your invitation: names, event dates, times and venues, photos, music, a message for guests, and the names and phone numbers of the people guests can call. Only add other people's details with their permission.
  • From guests: the name and message of any wish they write in the guest book. Guests don't create an account. The invitation keeps a copy of itself and the guest's language choice on their own phone so it opens faster next time.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to make and show your invitation, to help you with it, and to keep our accounts. We don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Who can see an invitation

Anyone who has its link can open it while it is open, and read the wishes you haven't hidden. Invitations aren't listed or searchable on our site. Only you (with your PIN) and we can change an invitation or see hidden wishes.

Where it is stored

Invitations, photos, music and orders are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). The pages are served by our hosting provider. When you type English text in the editor, it is sent to Google's Gemini service to be translated into Tamil.

How long we keep it

  • An invitation is open to guests until 15 days after the event's date, unless agreed otherwise.
  • About 3 months after it closes, we delete its photos, music and wishes.
  • We keep the order record (name, phone, amount, payment reference) for as long as the law requires for accounts.

Your choices

You can change or remove anything on your invitation yourself at any time. To have your invitation or your details deleted sooner, or to correct them, contact us and we will do it.
