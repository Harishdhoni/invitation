Questions

Can I change the details after I share the link?

Yes. Change anything in your editor at any time; guests see the new details the next time they open the link.

How long does the link work?

Until 15 days after your event's date. Ask us if you'd like it open longer.

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. The invitation opens in any phone's browser, straight from WhatsApp.

Is it in Tamil?

Every text is in Tamil and English. Guests choose with one tap. Names and places are written in Tamil script for you.

Can I have a version without our phone numbers?

Yes. Make up to three links, each in its own design, and choose what each one shows: contact numbers, countdown, guest book, music and more.

Who can see my invitation?

Only people who have the link. It isn't listed anywhere. We delete photos, music and wishes about three months after the invitation closes. See our privacy policy.
